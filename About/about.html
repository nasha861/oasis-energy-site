About Oasis Energy

Oasis Energy is committed to delivering clean, reliable, and affordable solar solutions for homes and businesses across Nigeria.

Our Mission

To provide sustainable and cost-effective solar energy solutions that empower individuals and businesses to reduce energy costs and environmental impact.

At Oasis Energy, our mission is to democratize access to renewable energy. We believe every household and business deserves affordable, reliable, and sustainable power solutions.

We are committed to reducing dependence on fossil fuels and lowering carbon footprints through innovative solar technologies.

Our mission extends beyond installation — we educate communities, empower individuals, and foster resilience in the face of energy challenges.

We design systems tailored to unique needs, ensuring maximum efficiency and long‑term savings.

Ultimately, our mission is to accelerate the transition to a cleaner, brighter future powered by the sun.

Our Vision

To become a leading renewable energy provider in Africa, driving innovation and making solar energy accessible to all.

We envision a world where renewable energy is the foundation of modern living. Our vision is to see communities thrive on solar power, reducing emissions and protecting the planet.

We aim to lead innovation in solar technology, setting new standards for efficiency and durability.

Our vision includes empowering individuals to become energy producers, reshaping the way society interacts with power.

We aspire to inspire a global movement toward clean energy adoption through advocacy and education.

Through partnerships and innovation, we see a future where solar energy drives sustainable growth and prosperity.

Meet the Visionary

Oasis Energy was founded by a visionary leader who saw the urgent need to shift from fossil fuels to renewable energy. His journey began with a passion for sustainability and a determination to make solar accessible to all.

He envisioned a company that would not only install panels but also educate communities, empower individuals, and inspire change. His vision was rooted in the belief that energy independence is a right, not a privilege.

Driven by personal experiences of unreliable power and rising costs, he committed to building solutions that are affordable, durable, and scalable.

His leadership has shaped Oasis Energy into a trusted partner for thousands of households and businesses, delivering clean energy with integrity and innovation.

Today, his vision continues to guide our mission, inspiring us to push boundaries and redefine what’s possible in renewable energy.

Through his foresight, Oasis Energy stands as a beacon of hope, proving that one person’s vision can ignite a movement toward a sustainable future.

Engr. Aishat Raji
